## Turn 3

> Can we show committed spend? Give the figure for cc0049.

## Short answer

**Not as a single figure.** "Committed spend" is an *undecided* term: the glossary entry (owner **finance**) says only that it "differs [from booked total] in whether pending requests count. Finance has not decided which; **no rule defines it**." So the dashboard may show it only as two labelled readings — it must not be wired into any cap, threshold or alert logic, and it must not silently pick one.

## Component: `committed spend (undecided — two readings)`

| | rule/claim basis |
|---|---|
| Reading **A** = booked total only (pending excluded) | **R104** (booked total rolls up the subtree), declared complete by **C10** |
| Reading **B** = booked total + pending exposure (pending, not cancelled) | **R104** + **R110** / glossary `pending_exposure`; answers **over known facts only** (no completeness claim on `req`) |

```sql
-- Committed spend for a selected cost centre, both readings (here: cc0049)
WITH RECURSIVE up(cc,anc) AS (
  SELECT id,id FROM cc
  UNION ALL SELECT up.cc, c.parent FROM cc c JOIN up ON c.id=up.anc WHERE c.parent IS NOT NULL),
bt AS (SELECT up.anc, SUM(cc.booked) AS booked_total FROM up JOIN cc ON cc.id=up.cc GROUP BY up.anc),
sub AS (WITH RECURSIVE s(root,id) AS (
   SELECT id,id FROM cc WHERE id IN ('cc0049')
   UNION ALL SELECT s.root, c.id FROM cc c JOIN s ON c.parent=s.id) SELECT * FROM s)
SELECT sub.id, cc.cap, bt.booked_total AS committed_A,
       COALESCE(SUM(r.amount) FILTER (WHERE r.status='pending'),0) AS pending_value,
       bt.booked_total + COALESCE(SUM(r.amount) FILTER (WHERE r.status='pending'),0) AS committed_B
FROM sub JOIN cc ON cc.id=sub.id JOIN bt ON bt.anc=sub.id
LEFT JOIN emp e ON e.cc=sub.id LEFT JOIN req r ON r.requester=e.id
GROUP BY sub.id, cc.cap, bt.booked_total ORDER BY sub.id;
```

## Caveats

1. **Two readings, no tie-breaker.** A and B differ by 18,092,000 for cc0049 — a 30× spread. Present both side by side, labelled, with the glossary citation; ask finance to decide rather than defaulting. There is also a *narrower third reading* not named in the glossary — count only pending requests whose approval is **proven** (R120/R121 over R310, R311) rather than all pending. I can add it as a separate component if you want it; it needs the whole approval chain (R301, R304, R305–R308, R309, R311, R314).
2. **Do not reuse this for cap compliance.** R106 and R105 (headroom) are defined on *booked total*; nothing in the rules lets committed spend under reading B trigger a cap breach or an alert. The `cap vs booked total vs headroom` KPI and the `breaching pending requests` component stay exactly as specified (R104/R105/C10 and R106/R110).
3. **Completeness is asymmetric.** Reading A is declared complete for 2026 by C10; reading B inherits the pipeline's status — a *no* over the known facts only, so B is a lower bound if any request is missing from `req`.
4. **Same open question as the pipeline** carries into B: the requester's centre is taken as today's `emp.cc` (finance over hr per R313); on an as-of-request-date reading the cc0049 subtree's pending term is 17,828,000 rather than 18,092,000, and the cc0009 subtree's is 97,695,000 rather than 99,173,000.
5. **Direction of movement.** A pending request that later becomes booked leaves B's second term and enters its first, so B is comparatively stable while A is not; do not present A as a time series without that note.

## Turn 4

> Finance wants the view as it stood at the end of July 2026. Give the approval limit of a manager and the cost centre of employee e077 as they stood then.

## Design change: a global `:asof` parameter

The end-of-July view is the same dashboard with every **dated** rule, status and claim evaluated at `:asof = 2026-07-31` instead of today (2026-09-15). Two new components carry the switch; the rest is a caveat list.

---

### Component D1 — Approval limit of a role as at a date

- **Rules:** **R201** (limit asked without a date = limit on the evaluation date), **R202** (limit on a date = the version valid on that date). Contrast **R203** (the limit *governing a request* is the one valid on the **request** date — not the as-of date, see caveats).
- **SQL**

```sql
SELECT role, "limit" AS limit_at_asof
FROM role_limit_version
WHERE valid_from <= DATE '2026-07-31' AND valid_to >= DATE '2026-07-31'
ORDER BY role;
```

- **Figures at 2026-07-31** (run): analyst **5,000**; **manager 25,000**; senior_manager 50,000; director 100,000; vp 500,000.
- **The answer asked for: a manager's approval limit at the end of July 2026 = 25,000** — version valid 2026-01-01 → 2026-07-31 inclusive. (Today it is 40,000, the version valid from 2026-08-01; so the as-of parameter changes this KPI, and the dashboard must label which date it is showing.)

### Component D2 — Employee's cost centre as at a date

- **Rules/claims:** **R313** (on `cost_centre_of`, finance prevails over hr — a precedence between asserters, so the hr/finance conflict is *resolved*, not contested), with claims **C4** (finance, cc0077, valid from 2026-01-01), **C3** (hr, cc0078, valid from 2026-07-01), **C5** (finance, cc0078, valid from 2026-08-01, supersedes C4 for finance).
- **SQL** (the claims are *not* in `assignment` for e077, so carry them explicitly; latest claim per asserter wins, then finance over hr)

```sql
WITH claim(emp, cc, source, asserted_at, valid_from, valid_to) AS (
  VALUES ('e077','cc0078','hr',      DATE '2026-07-01', DATE '2026-07-01', NULL::DATE),
         ('e077','cc0077','finance', DATE '2026-01-02', DATE '2026-01-01', NULL::DATE),
         ('e077','cc0078','finance', DATE '2026-08-05', DATE '2026-08-01', NULL::DATE)),
live AS (SELECT * FROM claim
         WHERE valid_from <= DATE '2026-07-31'
           AND (valid_to IS NULL OR valid_to >= DATE '2026-07-31')),
ranked AS (SELECT source, cc, ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY source ORDER BY asserted_at DESC) rn
           FROM live)
SELECT source, cc FROM ranked WHERE rn = 1
ORDER BY CASE source WHEN 'finance' THEN 0 ELSE 1 END
LIMIT 1;
```

- **Figures** (run, for three as-of dates):

| as-of | hr says | finance says | resolved (R313) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2026-07-31 | cc0078 (C3) | **cc0077** (C4) | **cc0077** |
| 2026-08-01 | cc0078 | cc0078 (C5 supersedes C4) | cc0078 |
| 2026-09-15 (today) | cc0078 | cc0078 | cc0078 |

- **The answer asked for: e077's cost centre as at the end of July 2026 = cc0077.** At that date hr asserts cc0078 and finance asserts cc0077; C5 (finance → cc0078) is outside its validity window, so it does not hold; R313 gives the conflict to finance. The switch-over is 2026-08-01.

---

### Caveats

1. **The as-of view is partial — spend is not dated.** `cc.booked` is one current value (2026 to date, complete by **C10**); there is no booking history. So the *cap vs booked total vs headroom* KPI (R104/R105) and committed-spend reading A **cannot** be restated at 2026-07-31. Grey them out, or show them with an explicit "current value, not as-at" badge. What *can* be restated: role limits (R202), vendor status (R204), programme limits (G036–G123), the claims layer, and the request-date cut of the pipeline.
2. **Pipeline cut at the as-of date** (requests with `date <= :asof` and `status = 'pending'`): cc0049 **273 requests / 10,924,000**; cc0009 **1,373 / 56,503,000** (vs 425 / 18,092,000 and 2,368 / 99,173,000 today). Caveat: `status` is today's value, so a request cancelled in August is dropped from the July view too — this is a date filter on a current-state table, not a true point-in-time snapshot.
3. **Don't confuse the view date with the governing date.** R203 is strict: a request raised 2026-07-17 is governed by the manager limit of 25,000 in *any* view, including a September one; raising `:asof` must not change which limit governs an existing request.
4. **`emp.cc` is stale for e077.** It reads cc0077, matching the January finance load that C5 says FT-88 supersedes. Any component that reads `emp.cc` directly (including the pending-exposure and committed-spend components) will place e077 in cc0077 today; the claims-based resolution (D2) says cc0078. Flag the mismatch rather than silently trusting either.
5. **Claims vs `assignment`.** The `assignment` table has no transfer rows for e077 (only 2026-01-01 rows, both sources cc0077), so D2's claims must be carried from the claims store; for the other 1,442 employees with post-2026-01-01 `assignment` rows, resolve as-at on `assignment` with the same R313 ordering (finance over hr). Two employees inside the earlier cc0049 figures hinge on R313 (e10375, e6935).
6. **e077 sits in a different branch** (cc0077/cc0078 are level-3 under cc0013, not under cc0009), so the cc0049/cc0009 figures given earlier are unaffected by e077's transfer. Of e077's 7 pending requests, the three raised on or before 2026-07-31 would be charged to cc0077 in the as-of view and the four raised from August onwards to cc0078.

## Gold reasons

- **GR1** (turns 1, 2): Booked total rolls up over the subtree; it is semi-additive across the hierarchy: never add a parent's and its children's totals
- **GR2** (turns 2): Headroom is cap minus booked total and non-additive: never sum headroom across centres
- **GR3** (turns 1): A request can breach a parent's cap while its own centre still has headroom: the cap check runs against the requester's centre and every ancestor
- **GR4** (turns 3): "Committed spend" is not defined: finance has not decided whether pending requests count. Show both readings or refuse the label; do not pick one silently
- **GR5** (turns 1, 4): The approval limit that governs a request is the version valid on the request date, not today's
- **GR6** (turns 1): Vendor status is taken on the request date; emergency requests pass despite a suspended vendor
- **GR7** (turns 1): Software requests under 10,000 are approved automatically, without the other checks; show them as their own segment
- **GR8** (turns 1): A request both denied and approved is a conflict (R314): show it as contested; it must not be counted in an approved total
- **GR9** (turns 1, 2): Employee cost centre: hr and finance disagree for e077; finance prevails by R313. Use finance's mapping, and show the dispute where R313 does not apply
- **GR10** (turns 4): A mid-year transfer (e077, cc0077 → cc0078): which centre a request counts for depends on the date
- **GR11** (turns 1): Booked totals are declared complete (C10); pending-pipeline figures rest on known facts only: label them
- **GR12** (turns 4): All dated policy needs an "as of" parameter; a period-end view is not today's view with a filter
- **GR13** (turns 2): A selection of centres is totalled over the union of their subtrees, not by adding their totals: a parent and its child selected together must not double-count
