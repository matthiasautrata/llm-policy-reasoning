Read the paper below as a colleague forwarded it to you, with the note "worth your time?". You are the reader
described at the end of this prompt. Answer in your own words, briefly and honestly. Do not review the style or
suggest edits.

1. Your key take-away, in one or two sentences.
2. What did you learn that you did not know or believe before? If nothing, say so.
3. Would you care: would you act on it, or forward it? Why, or why not?
4. Where, if anywhere, did you have to work to find the point, or lose interest? Quote at most ten words to locate.
