# C1 -- committed spend: "committed spend" means spend already BOOKED. Requests still pending do not count towards it.

# C4 -- how caps apply up the tree: a request consumes its own centre's cap AND the cap of every ancestor centre; spend in a team therefore also counts against its division and against the company.
